# Review a logo-led product website

Inspect the rendered page in a browser. Check the actual animation driver—scrolling, playback or interaction—and the relevant desktop/mobile compositions. A syntax check or an agent's own verdict does not prove visual quality.

## What to judge

First pause on the important settled scenes at their actual on-page size. Can the subject be recognized without its heading? Are its distinctive contour, construction and important parts present and well related? Does the chosen flat, line or shaded treatment look intentionally finished? Fix a weak destination illustration before trying to improve its interpolation.

1. **Brand origin:** the visual grammar comes from this logo's geometry and character, not just its colors. Removing the header mark should not make the page indistinguishable from another product's template.
2. **Product meaning:** illustrations show real functions or uses. Logo-derived parts participate in their structure; they are not unrelated background decoration.
3. **Middle-frame quality:** sample the transition around its authored waypoints and halfway between them. No accidental knots, collapsed slabs, sharp joins, stretched type or unexplained visual debris.
4. **Continuity:** the intended shapes/objects survive the handoff. A transition can change technique, but should not quietly replace a promised morph with unrelated crossfades.
5. **Composition:** complete painted artwork, moving details and shadows leave room for copy/actions throughout the journey, not only at endpoints. Check incoming and outgoing content.
6. **Timing:** motion supports the reading order; resting states are clear. For scroll-linked work, check just before, at and after each chapter threshold: the visible copy and artwork should describe the same section, and the pinned stage should remain in range for the whole story. Playback/loops have intentional starts and endings. Reverse or interrupted movement behaves coherently where the chosen interaction allows it.
7. **Identity:** official-logo resting states match the supplied source, with no invented joins, cracks, doubled edges or altered lettering.
8. **Usability:** readable mobile composition, useful actions, no unwanted overflow or intercepted input. On phone widths, check fixed/sticky bars for clipped children as well as page-level overflow. Reduced motion retains a clear explanation and reachable controls.

Review representative stills for composition, and paced scrolling/playback or a recording for temporal quality. Screenshots alone cannot establish smoothness or FPS. If measuring performance, report the browser/device, workload and actual result.

Follow the source elements into the destination, including an internal stroke, mass or negative space when that is part of the concept. Check that the motion is more than a fading source mark plus a morphing generic background. Rich details can appear later, but should join the same construction.

When the page returns to the official logo, inspect that resting frame beside the supplied source asset. A similar-looking outline or interpolated approximation is not the original mark.

Classify the observed issue before changing numbers: missing illustration structure calls for drawing; inaccurate boundaries call for geometry/correspondence work; uneven temporal motion calls for timing/rendering work. Use the illustration-craft comparison for the first category, without treating its style as mandatory.

Fix the observed problem in its geometry, choreography, asset or layout. Review the changed sequence and nearby states afterward; a minor revision does not call for a new full application test suite.

## Validate the skill's generality when requested

Give an independent agent this skill, a **different product with a structurally different logo**, its actual assets and an isolated output directory. Let it derive the page and motion without supplying the old site's intended layout or your expected solution.

Inspect the resulting artifact. If it reproduces the old brand's chapter structure, feature subjects or component count without a product-based reason, improve the design instructions rather than adding more example-specific tuning constants.

Record which brief was actually built and inspected. A successful reproduction of the original site demonstrates reuse, not cross-brand generality. If another agent/tool is unavailable, state that limit and deliver the authorized skill changes without inventing an independent pass.

When comparing a skill revision on the same product, keep the model, raw brand inputs and requested scope comparable. Start a fresh session and let the skill carry the drawing method; do not supply the previous solution or secretly redraw the result yourself. Inspect illustration fidelity as well as page correctness, and state any changed execution conditions that limit the comparison.
