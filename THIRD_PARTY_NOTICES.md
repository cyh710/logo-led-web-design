## Noto Sans SC and Noto Serif SC

The optional Tapestry reference site bundles subset WOFF2 files for Noto Sans SC and Noto Serif SC in `assets/reference-site/assets/`. Each font's SIL Open Font License 1.1 text is included beside the font. Those license files govern the font software and take precedence over the repository's MIT license for those files.

The font metadata and upstream projects are documented by [Google Fonts: Noto Sans SC](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/notosanssc), [Google Fonts: Noto Serif SC](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/notoserifsc), and [Noto CJK](https://github.com/notofonts/noto-cjk).

The bundled reference-site logo and illustration assets are original Tapestry project materials, included to demonstrate the skill's method; the case study is optional and should not be copied into unrelated products.
